# دروب (Duroob) — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** «د» دروب drawn as board tiles, one per right answer; the runner is the next step.
- **Construction:** the Kufi letter dal is six rounded-square tiles (3 on top, 1 on the right, 2 on the bottom row) and a solid circle, the runner, in the empty bottom-left slot.
- **Versions:** horizontal lockup (primary) · stacked lockup · symbol only · wordmark only · small-size cut of the symbol.
- **Reading order:** the lockup reads right to left. The symbol stands on the right and the name follows to its left. The baseline of the name sits on the bottom tile row.
- **Two colour schemes:** each comes as a file:
  - `color-dark`: limestone tiles, lantern runner, limestone name. Use it on night or other dark backgrounds.
  - `color-light`: night tiles, dark-lantern runner, night name. Use it on limestone, white or other light backgrounds.
- **Files:**
  - `masters/`: colour SVG masters (symbol, symbol-small, horizontal, stacked, wordmark; each in both schemes).
  - `symbol/`: black, white, mono, square, favicon and app-icon versions, SVG and PNG at 512 and 1024.
  - `lockups/`: black, white and mono lockups and wordmark, SVG and PNG at 1200.
  - `web/`: `favicon.ico`, PNG icons, `site.webmanifest` and `head-snippet.html`.

## 2. Clear space
The unit is **x = half a tile** (25 units in the 256 grid), which is 14 % of the symbol's width.
- Keep **2x** clear on every side of any version.
- The gap between the symbol and the name inside a lockup is also 2x. Don't change it.
- The name's letter body is 47 % of the symbol's height.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 48 px tall | 12 mm tall |
| Stacked lockup | 96 px tall | 20 mm tall |
| Symbol | 32 px | 8 mm |
| Symbol, small cut (`symbol-small-*`: bigger tiles, wider gaps) | 16–31 px | 4–8 mm |

Below 48 px tall, the name in the horizontal lockup is too small to read. Use the symbol alone. At 16 px the gaps between tiles stay at about 1.5 px in the small cut.

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | #1a1714 | 26 23 20 | 0 12 23 90 |
| Limestone | #efe6d4 | 239 230 212 | 0 4 11 6 |
| Lantern (runner on dark) | #e8a33d | 232 163 61 | 0 30 74 9 |
| Dark lantern (runner on light only) | #a8661a | 168 102 26 | 0 39 85 34 |

The CMYK values are converted from RGB. Get a printer's proof, or have a Pantone match chosen, before printing.

**Contrast (WCAG):**
- Lantern on night: 8.3 : 1
- Limestone on night: 14.4 : 1
- Dark lantern on white: 4.6 : 1
- Dark lantern on limestone: 3.7 : 1 (fine for a graphic shape, not for small text)
- Lantern on limestone: **1.7 : 1. Never use this pair.** It is why light backgrounds use dark lantern.

**One-colour versions:** black, white, mono night (#1a1714), mono lantern (#e8a33d). The runner stays a separate dot, because it has its own slot.

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- `color-dark` on night.
- `color-light` on limestone or white.
- Lantern one-colour on night.
- Night one-colour on limestone or white.
- White on any dark solid colour.
- Black on white.

## 5. Typography
- **The name** is drawn as outlines from **Reem Kufi Bold**. Its licence (SIL Open Font License 1.1) allows logo use, and a copy ships as `OFL.txt`. Never retype the name: always use the files.
- **Text set next to the logo:** use Reem Kufi for display text. For body text, use the game's own UI stack.

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't swap the tile and runner colours, or put `color-light` on night (the night tiles disappear).
- Don't use lantern on limestone.
- Don't stretch, squash, rotate or mirror the logo.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects.
- Don't move, resize or swap the symbol and the name inside a lockup.
- Don't join the tiles or touch the runner to them: the gaps make it a path.
- Don't add or remove tiles, and don't add any other shape to the mark.

## 7. Master files
The SVG masters are in `masters/`. They are generated by `build_kit.py`, which uses the drawn name in `name.json`:
`python3 build_kit.py name.json masters`
