# حصون المعرفة — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** an open book whose page tops are battlements. Knowledge is the wall: حصون (fortresses) + المعرفة (knowledge).
- **Versions:** horizontal lockup (primary) · stacked lockup · symbol only · wordmark only.
- **Reading order:** the lockup reads right to left. The symbol stands on the right and the name follows to its left.
- **Two colour schemes:** each comes as a file:
  - `color-dark`: brass and parchment pages, parchment name. Use it on navy or other dark backgrounds.
  - `color-light`: navy and dark-brass pages, navy name. Use it on parchment, white or other light backgrounds.
- **Files:**
  - `masters/`: colour SVG masters.
  - `symbol/`: one-colour symbol, square and app-icon versions, PNGs at 512 and 1024.
  - `lockups/`: one-colour lockups and wordmark, SVG and PNG at 1200.
  - `web/`: favicon, web icons, `site.webmanifest` and `head-snippet.html`.

## 2. Clear space
The unit is **x = the width of one battlement** (the merlon on top of a page). That is 7.3 % of the symbol's width.
- Keep **2x** clear on every side of any version.
- The gap between the symbol and the name inside a lockup is also 2x. Don't change it.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 48 px tall | 12 mm tall |
| Stacked lockup | 96 px tall | 20 mm tall |
| Symbol | 32 px | 8 mm |
| Symbol, small cut (`symbol-small-*`, 2 battlements a page) | 16–31 px | 4–8 mm |

Below 48 px tall, the name in the horizontal lockup is too small to read. Use the symbol alone.

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navy (war room) | #0e1624 | 14 22 36 | 61 39 0 86 |
| Brass | #c9a24a | 201 162 74 | 0 19 63 21 |
| Dark brass (light backgrounds only) | #8a6a22 | 138 106 34 | 0 23 75 46 |
| Parchment | #f4ecd8 | 244 236 216 | 0 3 11 4 |

The CMYK values are converted from RGB. Get a printer's proof, or have a Pantone match chosen, before printing.

**Contrast (WCAG):**
- Brass on navy: 7.6 : 1
- Parchment on navy: 15.4 : 1
- Navy on parchment: 15.4 : 1
- Dark brass on parchment: 4.3 : 1
- Dark brass on white: 5.0 : 1
- Brass on parchment: **2.0 : 1. Never use this pair.** It is why light backgrounds use dark brass.

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- `color-dark` on navy.
- `color-light` on parchment or white.
- Brass one-colour on navy.
- Navy one-colour on parchment or white.
- White on any dark solid colour.
- Black on white.

## 5. Typography
- **The name** is drawn as outlines from **Reem Kufi Bold**. Its licence (SIL Open Font License 1.1) allows logo use, and a copy ships as `OFL.txt`. Never retype the name: always use the files.
- **Text set next to the logo:** use Reem Kufi for display text. For body text, use the game's own UI stack.

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't swap the page colours, or put `color-light` on navy (the navy page disappears).
- Don't use regular brass on parchment.
- Don't stretch, squash, rotate or mirror the logo.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects.
- Don't move, resize or swap the symbol and the name inside a lockup.
- Don't close the gap between the pages: it is what makes the shape read as a book.

## 7. Master files
The SVG masters are in `masters/`. They are generated by `build_kit.py`, which uses the drawn name in `name.json`.
