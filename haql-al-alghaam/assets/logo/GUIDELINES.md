# حقل الألغام (Haql al-Alghaam) — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** the mine's body is a field square: any square might be this one. The «tile shell» is a rounded-square tile with a gloss arc cut out of it, a fuse growing from its corner and a flame at the tip.
- **Construction:** body = rounded square (160 units, corner radius 34); gloss = a curved band cut out of the body (a real hole); fuse = a curved band of width 16; flame = a teardrop. The whole mark is centred in the 256 box.
- **Versions:** horizontal lockup (primary) · stacked lockup · symbol only · wordmark only · small-size cut of the symbol.
- **Reading order:** the lockup reads right to left. The symbol stands on the right and the name follows to its left. The baseline of the name sits on the bottom edge of the body.
- **Two colour schemes:**
  - `color-dark`: sand body and fuse, flame-orange flame, sand name. Use it on field night or other dark backgrounds.
  - `color-light`: field-night body, fuse and name, ember flame. Use it on sand, white or other light backgrounds.
- **Files:**
  - `masters/`: colour SVG masters (symbol, symbol-small, horizontal, stacked, wordmark; each in both schemes).
  - `symbol/`: black, white, mono, square, favicon and app-icon versions, SVG and PNG at 512 and 1024.
  - `lockups/`: black, white and mono lockups and wordmark, SVG and PNG at 1200.
  - `web/`: `favicon.ico`, PNG icons, `site.webmanifest` and `head-snippet.html`.
  - `build_kit.py` + `name.json` regenerate `masters/`; `overview.png` shows the set.

## 2. Clear space
The unit is **x = the fuse width** (16 units in the 256 grid), about 8 % of the symbol's width.
- Keep **2x** clear on every side of any version.
- The gap between the symbol and the name inside a lockup is also 2x. Don't change it.
- The name's tallest letter (alif/lam) is 58 % of the symbol's height.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 48 px tall | 12 mm tall |
| Stacked lockup | 96 px tall | 20 mm tall |
| Symbol | 32 px | 8 mm |
| Symbol, small cut (`symbol-small-*`: no gloss arc, thicker fuse, bigger flame) | 16–31 px | 4–8 mm |

Below 48 px tall the name in the horizontal lockup is too small to read: use the symbol alone. At 16 px the small cut keeps the flame and the fuse visible and separate (gaps of at least 1 px).

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field night | #23291c | 35 41 28 | 15 0 32 84 |
| Sand | #ece0c4 | 236 224 196 | 0 5 17 7 |
| Flame (on dark) | #ef6a2e | 239 106 46 | 0 56 81 6 |
| Ember (flame on light only) | #b8461a | 184 70 26 | 0 62 86 28 |

CMYK values are converted from RGB. Get a printer's proof, or a Pantone match, before printing.

**Contrast (WCAG):**
- Sand on field night: 11.4 : 1
- Flame on field night: 4.8 : 1
- Field night on white: 14.9 : 1
- Field night on sand: 11.4 : 1
- Ember on white: 5.3 : 1
- Ember on sand: 4.1 : 1 (fine for a graphic shape, not for small text)
- Flame on sand: **2.4 : 1. Never use this pair.** It is why light backgrounds use ember.

**One-colour versions:** black, white, mono field night (#23291c), mono flame (#ef6a2e).

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- `color-dark` on field night.
- `color-light` on sand or white.
- Flame one-colour on field night.
- Field-night one-colour on sand or white.
- White on any dark solid colour.
- Black on white.

## 5. Typography
- **The name** is drawn as outlines from **Reem Kufi Bold (700)**. Its licence (SIL Open Font License 1.1) allows logo use; a copy ships as `OFL.txt`. Never retype the name: always use the files.
- **Text next to the logo:** Reem Kufi for display text; the game's own UI stack for body text.

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't add spikes, rays, crosses, plus shapes or crossing lines to the shell. It stays a plain rounded square.
- Don't swap the body and flame colours, or put `color-light` on field night (the body disappears).
- Don't use flame on sand.
- Don't stretch, squash, rotate or mirror the logo.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects.
- Don't move, resize or swap the symbol and the name inside a lockup.
- Don't fill in the gloss arc in the main cut, or use the small cut above 31 px.
- Don't add any other shape to the mark.

## 7. Master files
The SVG masters in `masters/` are generated by `build_kit.py` from the drawn name in `name.json`:
`python3 build_kit.py name.json masters`
