The brief

A host-run team quiz game in Arabic: right answers pay for battalions, every attack is a question, and losing your capital knocks you out. Played on a dark war-room table in navy and brass.

strategiclearnedtensecommunal

Says both halves of the name (fortress + knowledge), avoids quiz clichés (question marks, light bulbs), holds at 16 px, works in one colour on navy and parchment.

Final design

Book Wall

An open book whose page tops are battlements: knowledge is the wall.

  • Draws the name itself: حصون (fortress) + المعرفة (knowledge)
  • Two-tone pages keep the open book legible down to 16 px
  • Brass on navy is the game's own war-room palette
  • Kufic wordmark (Reem Kufi) echoes the square battlements
